Language

AgentsDock is available in English and Simplified Chinese. Pick the one you want, or let the app follow your system language.

Change the app language

  1. Open Settings (⌘, on Mac, Ctrl, on Windows and Linux).
  2. In the General section, find Language.
  3. Choose Follow system, English, or 简体中文.

Follow system matches the language your operating system is set to, so the app changes with it.

Supported languages

  • English
  • 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese)

This documentation is available in both languages too — use the EN / 中文 switch at the top of the sidebar.
