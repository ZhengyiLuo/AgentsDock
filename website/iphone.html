Install on iPhone & iPad

AgentsDock for iPhone and iPad is currently in beta through TestFlight — Apple's app for trying pre-release apps. It's two quick steps, and it's coming to the App Store soon.

The Mac, Windows, and Linux apps are direct downloads and don't need TestFlight — grab them from the downloads section. This page is just for iPhone and iPad while they're in beta.

1Get the TestFlight app

On your iPhone or iPad, install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store (if you don't already have it). TestFlight is how Apple distributes beta apps.

Get TestFlight on the App Store →

2Install AgentsDock

On the same device, open the AgentsDock TestFlight invite below, then tap Accept and Install. AgentsDock installs like a normal app.

Open the AgentsDock TestFlight invite →

Requires iOS / iPadOS 17 or later.

3Connect and start

Open AgentsDock, add your server, and start chatting. New here? Follow the Setup guide to get a server running, then start your first chat.

Beta builds update automatically through TestFlight, so you'll always be on the latest version. Once AgentsDock is on the App Store, you'll be able to install it directly — no TestFlight needed.
