Install on iPhone & iPad

AgentsDock for iPhone and iPad is on the App Store. Install it like any other app, connect it to your server, and you're chatting in a minute.

The Mac, Windows, and Linux apps are direct downloads — grab them from the downloads section. This page is just for iPhone and iPad.

1Install from the App Store

On your iPhone or iPad, get AgentsDock from the App Store and open it.

Download AgentsDock on the App Store →

Requires iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later.

2Connect and start

Open AgentsDock, add your server, and start chatting. New here? Follow the Setup guide to get a server running, then start your first chat.

App updates are delivered through the App Store. Automatic installation depends on your device settings.
